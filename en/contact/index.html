Updated on August 28, 2026

The official contact channel for Cortexplor is adm@cortexplor.com.

What to send

  • Correction: send the story link and the specific point to fix. These get priority.
  • Story tip: tell us the fact and where it was published or announced.
  • Press and releases: welcome, but a release alone is never a story — it needs independent confirmation.
  • Advertising: we monetise through ad networks and do not sell sponsored content.

Response time

We read everything. Corrections are answered within two business days; other subjects as the newsroom's schedule allows.
