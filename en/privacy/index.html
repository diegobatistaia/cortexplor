Privacy Policy

Updated on August 28, 2026

This policy explains how Cortexplor (cortexplor.com) handles information about people who visit the site.

What we collect

Reading the site requires no account, and we do not collect names or identity documents. We collect aggregate browsing data only: pages viewed, time on page, referral source, device type and approximate country.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site performs. It stores cookies in your browser and processes IP addresses in anonymised form. You can block these cookies in your browser settings or with Google's official opt-out extension.

Advertising

This site shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalised advertising in your Google ad settings.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we store only the e-mail address you provide, used solely to send the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link at the bottom of every issue.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at adm@cortexplor.com. We reply within 15 days.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page, with the update date at the top.
