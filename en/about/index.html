About Cortexplor

Updated on August 28, 2026

Cortexplor is a news site covering Artificial Intelligence: model releases, market moves, regulation and the practical use of AI inside companies, in Brazil and worldwide.

How our stories are produced

Our editorial production is AI-assisted and runs on a fixed verification process. Every story follows the same path:

  • Collection of recent reporting from established news sources;
  • Cross-checking: a fact only enters the text if at least two mutually independent sources confirm it;
  • Original writing from the confirmed facts — never a copy or a close paraphrase of the source text;
  • Automated checks on originality, structure and source citation before publication.

Stories that fail any of these steps are not published: they are held for human review or dropped.

Transparency

Every story lists the sources used. When sources disagree on a number or a detail, we say so in the text instead of hiding it.

Corrections

Found an error? Write to adm@cortexplor.com with the link. Meaningful corrections are flagged on the page itself.
