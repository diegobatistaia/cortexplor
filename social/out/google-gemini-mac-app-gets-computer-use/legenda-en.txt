Gemini's Mac app gained Computer Use and now operates other programs on the user's behalf.

Per SiliconANGLE, users include @Computer use in a natural-language prompt, and Gemini finds the app it needs, builds a plan and carries out the work step by step. Engadget says the feature was already on Windows and now reaches the native Mac app.

The assistant sees the screen through screenshots and clicks, types and navigates in the background without taking the user's cursor, per Engadget. Users choose which apps Gemini can reach and approve key actions. SiliconANGLE notes Meta and OpenAI already offer similar tools.
