Google launched Playground, an AI platform that builds custom games from text prompts with no coding.

Google says games built in Playground work on phone and laptop, and games in select genres already support leaderboards and multiplayer. With a Play Games profile, users can claim a handle, favorite games and follow other creators.

Google also plans a Unity Spark integration for creators who want to go further than Playground offers today. The company says games made with it will stay available to everyone using Playground, not only to users with access to the professional-grade tools.

Google describes the service as "an early experiment" and invites users to share games and feedback.
