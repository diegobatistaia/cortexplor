OpenAI updated the ChatGPT extension for Chrome: the assistant can now read open tabs and carry out tasks.

According to Canaltech, Olhar Digital, Tecmundo and Engadget, the extension used to answer only about the single page in view. Now Chat mode compares information across tabs, and Work mode fills out forms and schedules events.

The update is open to every ChatGPT plan, per Canaltech and Engadget. Task features also need the desktop app installed. Olhar Digital reports the system asks for authorization on new sites and for confirmation on sensitive actions, such as downloading files.
