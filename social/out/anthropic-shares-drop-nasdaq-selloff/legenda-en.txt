Anthropic shares sink on day two and drag the Nasdaq lower.

Anthropic shares fell 9% on Friday and closed at $88.30, near the $83 IPO price, according to the Guardian. On day one the stock had closed up 17% (The Guardian, CNBC). The drop came in a broad selloff: Nvidia lost 14% and SoftBank fell 14.5% in Tokyo (The Guardian).

The Nasdaq erased its 2026 gains, and the Dow and S&P 500 entered correction territory (The Guardian). Analyst Daniela Hathorn says AI demand is strong, but doubts over valuations and spending persist. Dario Amodei defended $900 billion in commitments (CNBC).

Is this a healthy correction for AI stocks or the start of a bigger slide?
