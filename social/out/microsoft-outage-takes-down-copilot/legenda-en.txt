A global outage took Copilot and Outlook down for more than 24 hours.

Per El País Tecnología and Xataka, the failure also hit Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive. Microsoft blamed a technical problem after a network expansion and denied any link to attacks or AI agents. Exchange Online went down first and overloaded the services tied to it, per El País Tecnología.

Xataka logged reports in over 70 countries on Downdetector, naming Maersk, UK hospitals and Stanford University among those hit. Microsoft chose to restore service gradually, which prolonged the outage. Xataka notes AWS, Azure and Google Cloud hold over 70% of Europe's cloud market.
