Google DeepMind used AI to mathematically prove parts of the Signal protocol's security.

Per Wired and ZDNet, the AlphaProof Code system formally verified cryptographic guarantees of the protocol used by Signal, WhatsApp and Google Messages. The work took about four weeks, with humans deciding what to prove and agents running Gemini Carbon.

Wired says the analysis found no new flaws but did flag old bugs in versions fixed long ago. Per ZDNet, only 1% of the code needed a second human check. DeepMind plans to open-source the system, and Wired reports the Signal proof will also be released. The full app was not covered.
