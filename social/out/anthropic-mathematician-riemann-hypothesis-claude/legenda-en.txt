An Anthropic mathematician claims a Riemann Hypothesis proof made with Claude.

Levent Alpöge says he proved the Riemann Hypothesis, a Millennium Prize problem, with help from Claude, according to The Verge, SiliconANGLE and Engadget. He posted it on his own site, and Anthropic has not commented. The proof reportedly runs more than 250,000 pages and is written in Lean, a language that checks each step (SiliconANGLE, Engadget).

Keep in mind: the claim has not been independently verified, and mathematicians outside Anthropic still need to review the proof (The Verge, Engadget). Until then it is an allegation, not a confirmed result.
