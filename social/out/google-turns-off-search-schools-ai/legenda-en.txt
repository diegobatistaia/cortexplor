Google confirmed US schools that restrict AI will also lose Search on student accounts.

Per The Verge and SiliconANGLE, Gemini and Search are off by default on K-12 accounts and only return if school admins turn them on. There is no option to disable only the AI. Google says search and AI are deeply integrated.

Bernie Sanders called the practice extortion and Lina Khan said the company is trying to coerce schools into adopting AI, per The Guardian. Google says it gives schools more control and points to Guided Learning, being tested in select schools.
