Jensen Huang will testify before the US Senate on December 3 after returning to Nvidia.

According to TechCrunch, Jensen Huang is scheduled to testify before the Senate Banking Committee on December 3 about AI chip export controls to China. CNBC says he is expected to appear before Congress twice in December, and TechCrunch lists another House hearing on December 1 (TechCrunch AI, CNBC Technology).

The stock jumped about 31% the day he returned and was down about 5% premarket on Tuesday (CNBC Technology). Investigations continue, and analysts are split on governance and remaining risks, per CNBC. Jim Cramer's view on the stock's price is his own opinion, not a consensus.
