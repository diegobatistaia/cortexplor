Jensen Huang said he would be honored to return as Nvidia CEO if the board decides so.

Per CNBC Technology, TechCrunch AI and The Verge AI, it was Huang's first public statement since resigning. He said he would answer those calls if the board concludes a return serves shareholders, employees and the company, admitted personal mistakes and promised to cooperate with the independent investigation.

CNBC Technology cited a source saying the board is not weighing a reinstatement for now. Per Engadget, Nvidia created the National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee, led by Suzanne Nora Johnson, meeting at least quarterly. Huang is still due to testify before Congress on December 1.
