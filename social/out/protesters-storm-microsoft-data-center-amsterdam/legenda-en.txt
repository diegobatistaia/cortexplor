Protesters stormed the site of a Microsoft data center near Amsterdam.

Per The Verge and TecMundo, around 100 people got onto the building site. Some climbed a crane and hung signs against AI and against Microsoft, tying the company to the war in Gaza. The Verge names the collective Geef Tegengas among the organizers.

The complex will have three 85-meter towers, for Microsoft alone. The plan dates to 2016, before the city's ban on new data centers through 2030, so work kept going. The Verge sees the protest as part of a wave across Europe, after another action in Dublin on Tuesday.
