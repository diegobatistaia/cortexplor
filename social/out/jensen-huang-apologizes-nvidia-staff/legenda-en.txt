Jensen Huang apologizes to Nvidia staff at his first all-hands since returning as CEO.

According to Wired and the Guardian, the executive admitted violating the company's code of conduct and described serious personal mistakes. CNBC, which spoke to people in the room, confirms the tone of the apology and says he mentioned going to therapy.

Huang also denied that Trump pressured the board to bring him back and called the formal succession process flawed (Wired AI). TechCrunch argues the bigger risk is cultural: employees may feel embarrassed even without formal punishment. Nvidia did not respond to requests for comment. The full story is at the link in bio.
