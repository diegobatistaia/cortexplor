ChatGPT GIF creation is here: it now makes and edits animated GIFs in the chat, with Images 2.6.

Per Canaltech, Tecmundo and Olhar Digital, users just describe the GIF or upload a photo. Then they can adjust speed, view frames and resize the file, something ChatGPT could not do natively before. The tool is built into the chat itself, so no separate app is needed.

OpenAI says the model generates images 50% faster, per Tecmundo. Two models reach the API, Flare and Sunburst. The feature covers all plans, with a gradual rollout, so it may not show up in every account yet.
