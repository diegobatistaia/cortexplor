Anthropic will put 5% of its shares into a new public benefit fund.

According to Anthropic, confirmed by TechCrunch, the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund will hold 5% of the shares, pending shareholder approval. For every 50% rise in valuation above a baseline, the board can add another 2 percentage points.

The company says the fund is for large donations to nonprofits and direct distributions to specific groups, such as people affected by AI in the labor market. It also says none of the fund's shares will go to lobbying or political giving. Governance and milestones will be detailed in the coming months.
