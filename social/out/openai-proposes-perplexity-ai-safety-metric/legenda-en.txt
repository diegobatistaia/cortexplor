OpenAI proposes perplexity as an AI safety metric.

According to OpenAI itself, chief scientist Jakub Pachocki wants perplexity, which measures how well a model predicts the next chunk of text, to become a shared industry standard for comparing progress and informing safety rules (OpenAI). The company published its trajectory going back to GPT-3.5.

The company says log-perplexity scores below 0.8 coincided with new capabilities in cybersecurity and math, and the forecast of new jumps at 0.68 to 0.70, maybe in 2027, is its own projection (OpenAI). It admits sharing the numbers helps rivals, but says a shared scientific basis matters more.
