Two pallets of RTX 5090 cards vanished on landing at Guarulhos airport, distributor Oderço says.

According to TecMundo and Canaltech, distributor Oderço puts the loss at about 2.5 million reais at cost price. The company says it does not know what caused the disappearance and did not say how many cards were in the shipment.

GRU Airport told TecMundo it reported the case to authorities and gave compensation instructions on October 14. According to Canaltech, the RTX 5090 averages 35,000 reais at retail, up from under 15,000 reais a year ago, because of AI demand.
