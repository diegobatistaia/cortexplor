Meta brings its Muse AI agent inside Instagram.

According to TechCrunch and Engadget, Meta integrated Muse into Instagram direct messages, and the assistant also suggests organizing saved posts and Reels. The feature reaches iOS and Android users in a few countries, and the automatic suggestion starts only in the US and Canada (TechCrunch AI).

CNBC cites Forter Intelligence data saying Muse accounted for 51% of purchase orders placed by AI agents in one week (CNBC Technology). Engadget notes Meta is repeating its strategy of pushing a new product into apps that already have billions of users.
