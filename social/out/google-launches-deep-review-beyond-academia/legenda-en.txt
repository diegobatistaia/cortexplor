Google expanded Deep Review, which vetted research papers, to contracts and Google Docs files.

According to Google Research, Deep Review checks logical consistency and claims in complex documents, and now covers Google Docs, presentations, legal contracts and financial material. It grew out of a pilot with four computer science conferences.

Google frames the launch as a response to a verification bottleneck, citing more than 74,000 submissions to three top AI conferences this year. Higher-level judgments stay with humans. Google did not share the tool's price.
