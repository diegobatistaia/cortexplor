Anthropic updated its Claude usage policy to ban sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior toward the model.

The change comes amid a debate over whether AI models could be conscious. The Guardian notes CEO Dario Amodei has said he cannot rule it out, while Kyle Fish leads Anthropic's model welfare research (The Verge).

Engadget reports the election policy is now titled "Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes" and drops a blanket ban on personalized voter targeting that also blocked uses like translating voter guides. The Verge says Microsoft's AI code of conduct rejects the idea that models have welfare.
