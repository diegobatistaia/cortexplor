The US told federal agencies to remove DeepSeek AI from their systems and barred new purchases.

According to Convergência Digital and Olhar Digital, Homeland Security ordered the removal of DeepSeek products and barred third-party services built on the Chinese company's technology. The department cites the risk of sensitive data reaching China.

Olhar Digital notes that the Commerce Department and the Navy had already banned the tool, as had states such as New York, Tennessee and Virginia. DHS set no deadline for removal, and China's embassy had not commented.
