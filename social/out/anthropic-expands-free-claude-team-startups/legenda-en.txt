Anthropic expanded Claude for Startups, offering eligible companies a free year of Claude Team and $1,000 in API credits.

TechRadar did not name the venture capital funds behind the extra tier, so not every accepted startup will see the full package. Office hours cover practical use cases and technical limits for each business (TechRadar).

According to TechRadar, Anthropic says many fast-growing AI-native companies already joined the program, and that this next phase opens it up to more. Approved startups can also reach more users through their own connectors on Claude Marketplace.

Which part of the package would matter most to an early-stage AI startup: the seats, the credits or the support?
