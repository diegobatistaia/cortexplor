OpenAI now calls its largest data centers compute campuses, on the eve of the US midterms.

Per TechRadar and The Verge, the term appeared in the Grid Protectors Program announcement, which starts in Effingham County, Georgia, and pledges that the company pays for the costs it creates for local power grids.

Critics quoted by The Verge say a new name does not fix the impact of the buildout. Rival Anthropic made broader data center pledges. The Verge says OpenAI did not answer its request for comment on the rename.
