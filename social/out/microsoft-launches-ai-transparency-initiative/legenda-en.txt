Microsoft launched the AI Transparency Initiative to build open security standards for AI agents.

Per Microsoft Source, Satya Nadella made the announcement at Ignite. The company sums up the principle in one line: greater AI autonomy requires greater transparency. Today, it says, organizations can watch the activity around a model but cannot see what happens inside it.

Microsoft says it works with more than 40 partners, such as Accenture, Adobe, KPMG, Mastercard and Volvo Cars, and is expanding its role in a NIST standards initiative. The work will be built into Microsoft AI, Foundry and Copilot, but the company gave no timeline for availability.
