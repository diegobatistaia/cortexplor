US lawmakers asked Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang for a closed-door briefing.

Per CNBC and SiliconANGLE, Republicans want to hear from Huang about Nvidia's protocols against foreign espionage. A closed-door briefing is a private session with lawmakers, unlike a public hearing. The request follows the opening of an independent investigation by the company's board.

Congress's interest reflects Nvidia's role in the US-China dispute: its most advanced chips face export limits to the Chinese market. The board sees no sign that secrets or national security were compromised, and Huang promised to cooperate. The sources do not confirm a single date for the meeting.
