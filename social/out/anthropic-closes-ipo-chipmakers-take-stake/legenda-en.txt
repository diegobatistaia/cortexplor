Anthropic closed its IPO at about $119.5B with chipmakers taking a stake.

According to the company's own statement, Anthropic closed its IPO with the underwriters' option fully exercised, at $83.00 per share, and gross proceeds of about $93.5 billion (Anthropic). In parallel, it sold a separate share class to AMD, Broadcom, Google, Micron, Microsoft, Nvidia and TSMC, raising about $26.0 billion.

Combined, the company says it raised about $119.5 billion before fees and expenses (Anthropic). Class A shares trade on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, while Class N, sold only to the seven buyers, does not trade on an exchange.
