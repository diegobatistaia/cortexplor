xAI folded Grok, Cursor and X Premium into one subscription, from $8 to $200 a month.

According to xAI, the plan replaces the separate subscriptions and shares one usage pool between the chatbot and Cursor, the coding tool the company acquired in August (Engadget). Unused allowance in one app carries over to the other.

Current subscribers can keep their plan or switch with a prorated refund (xAI, Engadget). Team plans add single sign-on, admin controls and SOC 2 compliance. xAI says it will give notice before moving people who do not switch, but set no date.
