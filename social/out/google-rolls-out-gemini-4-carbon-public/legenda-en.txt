Google DeepMind rolled out Gemini 4 Carbon to the public and launched the /swarm feature.

Per DeepMind, Google's strongest model was limited to cybersecurity specialists and now reaches developers, enterprises and consumers. It is in the Google AI Studio API and Gemini for Business, and Ultra plan subscribers find it in the Gemini app, AI Mode in Search and Antigravity.

/swarm, in testing in Antigravity, turns one prompt into a team of agents that check each other in parallel, per The Verge. The launch price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 for output, and it doubles in late November, according to Hipertextual. The Verge estimates Carbon costs about a fifth of GPT-6 Astra.
