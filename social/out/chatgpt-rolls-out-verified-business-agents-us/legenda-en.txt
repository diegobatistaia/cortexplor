ChatGPT is getting Business Agents, verified AI agents that represent companies.

Per The Verge and TechRadar, the feature is in beta and covers only eligible e-commerce and online-service businesses in the United States. The agent appears when a company shows up in an answer, and the chat with it stays separate from the main conversation.

To build an agent, a company signs up in the Business Hub, sets instructions and tracks performance metrics. TechRadar says chats are not shared with businesses and data arrives in aggregate. Per TechRadar, companies will be able to pay for more prominence, labeled as sponsored content.
