Anthropic launched Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra.

Per Fortune and TechCrunch, both models cost $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Fable 5.2 answers up to 30% faster than 5.1 and has a Fast mode that speeds up responses 2.5 times for double the price. Mythos 5.2 stays limited to vetted partners in cybersecurity and life sciences.

Fable 5.2 is live on Claude Platform on AWS, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud and Microsoft Azure. For routine tasks, Anthropic itself recommends Claude Opus 5.5 and keeps the new models for long, demanding technical work. Fortune notes that price weighs heavily in the fight with OpenAI for corporate customers.
