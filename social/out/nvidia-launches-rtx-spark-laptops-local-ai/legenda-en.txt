Nvidia is launching its first RTX Spark laptops this Friday, with up to 128GB of unified memory for local AI.

According to Nvidia, the platform pairs an Arm processor, a Blackwell GPU and 1 petaflop for AI workloads. When users first turn on the laptop, they pick an AI agent to install.

Engadget sees RTX Spark as a response to Apple Silicon chips. It debuts in Microsoft's Surface Laptop Ultra, among other partner models. The sources give no single price, and each manufacturer sets the price of its own laptop. Nvidia also says local model downloads are already five times all of last year.
