AWS, Anthropic, OpenAI and Google Cloud are racing to hire engineers who deploy AI inside client companies.

According to CNBC and TechRadar, AWS committed $1 billion and OpenAI launched a deployment business backed by over $4 billion. Anthropic is investing $100 million to train engineers at partner companies.

Lightcast data shows U.S. postings jumping from under 200 in 2023 to over 7,300 in 2026. CNBC reports the average advertised salary reached $195,456, more than three times the median posting in the country.
