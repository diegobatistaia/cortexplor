Anthropic OSS Scanner is a free tool that checks open-source code for flaws, with no human review.

According to The Verge and SiliconANGLE, projects that opt in get periodic scans, and reports come straight from Anthropic's strongest models, including Claude Mythos.

SiliconANGLE reports the service is funded by the Defender Advantage Fund, set up in August. The Verge notes that maintainers such as Linus Torvalds have reported struggling with the volume of AI-generated bug reports.

Anton Arapov of OpenSSL, an early tester, told SiliconANGLE that a report with a working exploit attached is "basically job done for an engineer."
