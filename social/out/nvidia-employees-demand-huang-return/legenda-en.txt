Nvidia employees asked, through internal channels, for Jensen Huang to return as CEO.

Per Wired as cited by Canaltech and Tecmundo, the notes appeared in the suggestion box and in company messengers, and target the board, which accepted the resignation. Canaltech says more than 1,600 employees left comments in the internal channel.

Tecmundo describes the messages as mostly anonymous and supportive of Huang. Per both outlets, some employees expected the independent investigation to finish before any change. Tecmundo sees a return as unlikely and notes Huang is still due to testify to US Congress.
