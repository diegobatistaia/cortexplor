Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia HQ to a cheering welcome.

According to videos shared with CNBC and TechCrunch, employees cheered, clapped and chanted Jensen Huang's name across three floors of Nvidia's headquarters. He thanked them for the "second chance" and promised to be a better CEO (CNBC Technology).

The petition for his return topped 200,000 signatures (TechCrunch AI, The Verge AI). ZDNet AI and SiliconANGLE compared the episode to Sam Altman's return to OpenAI. Shares recovered about $1 trillion in value on Monday, after the Nasdaq slid 10% the week before (CNBC Technology).
