Microsoft unveiled Maia 300, an AI inference chip it says delivers 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU systems.

Canaltech and TechRadar report that the chip arrives less than a year after the Maia 200, which debuted in January. TechRadar adds that each Maia 300 runs within a 950-watt thermal envelope, and a full 96-accelerator rack within a 167 kW power budget.

According to TechRadar, the accelerators link over high-bandwidth connections built for tensor parallelism, which splits one model's work across several chips, plus a custom AI transport layer on Ethernet.

Microsoft's announcement did not address pricing or outside sales, the outlets say.
