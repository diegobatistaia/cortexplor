Apple wants an AI agent that runs a computer on its own.

Apple posted and then pulled a listing for an engineer to lead an AI agent that can browse the web, use apps, run commands and write code, according to TechCrunch and The Verge. Bloomberg first spotted it. The role would sit inside the Siri and Foundation Models teams.

Meta, Google, OpenAI and Anthropic already have agents that take over computer tasks (TechCrunch). Meanwhile Apple is tightening third-party agent access to the Mac. Bloomberg found Apple considered a launch by the end of next year, but the project is still early (TechCrunch).

Should Apple build its own computer-using agent or stay out of the race?
