Microsoft unveiled Autopilot, an always-on agent, and Copilot Workspaces at Ignite.

Per ZDNet AI and SiliconANGLE, Autopilot stays active, watching email and Teams channels and taking over recurring tasks without anyone prompting it. Each agent has its own Microsoft Entra identity and a human manager, and IT teams can block it at any time.

In Copilot Workspaces, people and agents edit the same document, and it is available only in Frontier, Microsoft's early access program. The company also expanded Agent 365 with tools to measure and cap agent spending, per ZDNet AI.
