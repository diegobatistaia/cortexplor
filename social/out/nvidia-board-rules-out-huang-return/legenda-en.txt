Nvidia's board confirmed it does not plan to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO.

Per Engadget and TechCrunch, board chair Harvey Jones said on the earnings call that the board unanimously accepted the resignation and does not intend to reinstate Huang. The statement answered the request the former CEO made hours earlier, saying he would be honored to return.

In the same report, the first since the founder's exit, Nvidia posted revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% in a year and above the roughly $109 billion expected, per the Guardian. Colette Kress remains interim CEO, and the board is weighing internal and external candidates without rushing.
