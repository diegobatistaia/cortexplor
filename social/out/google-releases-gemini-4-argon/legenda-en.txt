Google released Gemini 4 Argon to developers and Pro and Ultra users, at $2 and $10 per million tokens.

According to The Verge and Estadão, that is about a fifth of what GPT-6 Astra and the Fable 5 models cost. The promotional rates hold through November and double in December.

Google says Argon beats Astra, Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 on its internal benchmarks. Security firm Wiz already uses it to find and fix vulnerabilities. Google has not said when it reaches Plus subscribers. The model supports up to 1 million combined tokens in a single task.
