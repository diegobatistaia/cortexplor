Facebook will let users type what they want to see in Feed and Reels.

According to Meta Newsroom, the "algorithm" panel lives in the three-dot menu on posts and accepts free text. People can list topics of interest and answer in their own words after tapping Interested or Not interested, and recommendations shift within the same session.

The company says it uses large language models to read what people write and blend it with likes, comments and watch history. Meta did not detail which countries have the feature or how the text is weighed against behavior.
