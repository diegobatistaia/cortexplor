Anthropic's founders cash out nearly $2B in the largest IPO ever.

Each of the seven co-founders sold about 3.4 million shares and took home roughly $280 million before taxes, according to TechCrunch and Fortune. Altogether that is about $1.95 billion (TechCrunch). They pledged 80% of their remaining equity to philanthropy, though the pledge is not legally binding (Fortune).

Despite a minority stake, the founders keep 50.1% of voting power (Fortune). The offering raised $81.3 billion on Nasdaq, the largest IPO on record (Wired), and Amazon and Google end up with a combined 28% of the company (TechCrunch, Fortune).
