OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said the world should accept hacks and scams as a cost of the benefits of artificial intelligence.

“I wouldn’t take a trade of saying we will make sure there’s no major hacks, there’s no misuse of this technology, there’s zero scams,” he said, according to The Guardian, SiliconANGLE and The Verge.

For The Verge, the remarks mark another divergence between OpenAI and Anthropic, which until recently appeared broadly aligned in calling for stronger rules for advanced AI. Altman also urged caution about humans losing control of AI systems.
