Google turned Gemini into an assistant that completes tasks, and the mode is free.

Per Google AI and TechCrunch AI, the new Tasks mode runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen to book appointments, manage bills and shop, connecting to a user's email, calendar and payment methods. It rolls out free in the US, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia and India.

Card payments through the Agent Payments Protocol start only in the US, with Mastercard and Visa, and users set a spending cap before approving, per Google AI. TechCrunch AI notes the Mac computer-use feature needs a paid plan and that Google trails OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta.
