OpenAI will test ads in ChatGPT image generation, shown beside the image while it is made, starting in the US this October.

The new placement builds on a fast rollout. According to Canaltech, OpenAI's first ad tests began in the US in February 2026, later reached Brazil, the UK, Mexico, Japan and South Korea, and expanded to 31 European countries in August. Olhar Digital reported ads also reached India that month.

In September OpenAI added sponsored agents, which open a conversation with a company-built system when a user follows an ad, plus AI tools to manage campaigns (Canaltech). Olhar Digital ties the push to rising competition, including Meta's free Muse app.
