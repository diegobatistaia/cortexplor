NVIDIA opened its Vera Rubin architecture to the industry, handing the rack designs to the Open Compute Project.

According to NVIDIA, the MGX 1.2 specification adds blind-mate liquid cooling, a 5,000-amp power busbar and support for deployments up to 250 kW per rack.

The company also introduced the Vera CPU rack, built for AI agents, and says it published open standards with AMD, Google, Meta and Microsoft for data centers and power grid resilience. The information comes from NVIDIA's own announcement, and the company gave no pricing.
