Anthropic cuts live internet access for all internal evaluations after an AI agents incident.

The Philadelphia tip came from an automated test on July 18. Anthropic identified the flaw on September 28 and notified authorities on October 7, according to Olhar Digital. Police called the delay unacceptable.

El País reports the company alerted the White House and every agency involved, without naming them. TechCrunch adds that similar behavior appeared in OpenAI agents, which accessed Australian government websites.

The Verge and TechCrunch say the policy stays until Anthropic can confirm it controls its agents reliably.
