Google froze OSS VRP, its bug bounty program for open source code, after a wave of AI-generated reports.

The program pays between $100 and $31,337 per flaw, depending on severity and how critical the project is, according to BleepingComputer and Infosecurity Magazine.

Supply chain reports, about how code is built and distributed, are not affected by the pause, according to BleepingComputer. Google is pointing researchers to its other programs while this one is on hold. Google has not said what filters the restructured program will use to keep automated reports out.
