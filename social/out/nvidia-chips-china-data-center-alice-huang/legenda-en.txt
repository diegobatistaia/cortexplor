Restricted Nvidia chips appear at a data center in China, according to documents cited by Bloomberg.

According to Engadget and Tecmundo, Shanghai Shuoyao pledged a GB300 rack as collateral for a loan of about $14 million. The hardware was allegedly installed at a supercomputing complex in Shanghai, where Bloomberg also found Hopper-generation servers.

The US Justice Department is investigating whether Megaspeed violated export rules, and the company denies wrongdoing (Engadget). Neither outlet explains how the chips reached China. Gigabyte said it follows the law, and Nvidia did not comment. The full story is at the link in bio.
