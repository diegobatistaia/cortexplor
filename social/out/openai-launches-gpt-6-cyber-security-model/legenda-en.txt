OpenAI launched GPT-6 Cyber, a security model it says goes off limits far less often.

Per The Verge, the model is better at finding vulnerabilities and reaches the top tier of Daybreak Red first, a program for security researchers. OpenAI raised its rating to Critical on the Preparedness Framework, up from High for GPT-5.6 Cyber.

The chance of acting off limits in tests fell from about 5% to under 1%, says The Verge. OpenAI says the model found 81 unknown flaws in Firefox, and TechCrunch reports it flagged 948 potential issues in the Linux kernel. The company also announced $1 billion for security professionals working in Daybreak.
