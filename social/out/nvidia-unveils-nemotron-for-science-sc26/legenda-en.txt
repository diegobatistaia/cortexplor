Nvidia unveiled Nemotron for Science, an open model built for research labs.

Per NVIDIA and SiliconANGLE, the announcement came at SC26 in Chicago. The model combines Nemotron 3.5 Super VL with an open chemistry dataset and post-training recipes, and can run locally, without relying on the company's cloud.

SiliconANGLE reports that Sweden's KTH used an agent built on the model to fix 81% of broken GROMACS setups in one night. Nvidia also presented the Rubin HPC GPU, planned for 2028, and a quantum computing partnership with IBM and Fermilab.
