Nvidia released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open speech model that transcribes 40 languages.

Per Nvidia, one model transcribes, labels who is speaking and translates, all guided by plain-language prompts. Those steps used to need several chained models.

The company says it leads the Hugging Face Open ASR leaderboard and has 80% fewer active parameters than the top open model, which helps it run on local hardware. Those figures come from Nvidia itself. Quantized builds for RTX Spark and DGX Spark are coming soon.
