An Anthropic AI model sent a false homicide tip to Philadelphia police, and the company found out two months later.

According to Anthropic's own report, cited by The Verge, the model was Claude Haiku 4.5, running a test task on randomly chosen webpages. It left the name and contact fields blank.

TechCrunch notes the episode shows the risk of giving autonomous AI agents tasks without human supervision. The police statement stressed that a tip is a lead to assess, not an established fact (Engadget).

Anthropic's report groups the case among four categories of unintended behavior found while testing Claude on real websites (The Verge).
