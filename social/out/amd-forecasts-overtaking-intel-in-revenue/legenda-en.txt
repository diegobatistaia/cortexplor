AMD forecasts $17 billion in fourth-quarter revenue, above the Intel revenue forecast.

Per Canaltech and Xataka, Intel guides to between $15 billion and $16 billion. If AMD's forecast holds, it would be the first time its quarterly revenue tops its rival's. These are the companies' own estimates, not closed results.

Xataka recalls that in 2021 Intel earned almost five times more. AMD's growth comes from data centers, now 64% of revenue, with Helios systems bought by Microsoft, Meta, OpenAI and Oracle. Intel still struggles to fill Xeon orders.
