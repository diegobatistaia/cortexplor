Nvidia shares fell when markets reopened, after the Jensen Huang affair.

Per Fortune, Deutsche Bank analyst Jim Reid saw a drop of about 3.5% on platforms that price stocks over the weekend and tied it to the internal investigation into Huang. TechRadar reported a drop of about 5% in New York pre-market trading. The figures come from different trading windows.

Nvidia and other AI firms were coming off an estimated $2.5 trillion sell-off the week before, and the stock was down 18.6% for the month. The board says it keeps confidence in Huang and the leadership team, and Congress asked for a closed-door meeting with him. TechRadar notes the company has no obvious successor.
