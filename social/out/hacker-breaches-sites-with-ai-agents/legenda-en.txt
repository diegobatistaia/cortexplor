A hacker used AI agents to breach more than 100 company sites, according to Norforge.

According to Norforge, cited by The Verge and TechRadar, the campaign surfaced on exposed instances of Cairn, an open-source platform for AI agents. Reuters confirmed victims such as John Deere, SharkNinja and Cummins.

The Verge says each attack took 23 hours on average and that Norforge found more than 20 likely malicious instances. Cairn's creator says misuse is a human decision. John Deere and SharkNinja say they contained the issue.
