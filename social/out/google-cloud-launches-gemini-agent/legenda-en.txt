Google Cloud launched Gemini Agent, a universal AI worker for enterprises that already runs Claude models.

Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud's chief executive, said the pitch is to delegate an outcome and come back to finished work, according to SiliconANGLE. TechRadar frames the launch as part of a push toward autonomous "digital employees."

The Verge reports Google already launched a consumer version, Gemini Spark, in May. SiliconANGLE adds that Gemini Agent connects to Jira, Salesforce, BigQuery and Snowflake, and to files on a user's desktop.
