Anthropic kicked off its IPO roadshow and wants to list on Nasdaq as ANT.

Per Anthropic itself, the company is offering 825 million Class A shares priced from $70 to $78 each. Of that total, 801,351,354 shares come from the company and the rest from existing stockholders, whom SiliconANGLE identifies as the seven co-founders.

At the top of the range, SiliconANGLE calculates the sale would total $64.35 billion, without a total market valuation. The banks have 30 days to buy up to 123.75 million extra shares, per Anthropic. SiliconANGLE says final pricing is expected on Nov. 18, if the SEC approves the registration.
