Roboflow launched action recognition for video, now tagging the start and end of each filmed step.

Per Roboflow, the computer vision platform, once built around object detection in single frames, now analyzes sequences of frames and returns a timeline of each detected action, with start and end times.

The company says two models power the feature, Cosmos 3 Edge and V-JEPA 2.1, and that the latter reached 87.3% accuracy in an internal gear assembly test, versus 80% for the former. The typical use case cited is monitoring production lines and robots. Training costs 2 credits per GPU hour, and V-JEPA 2.1, in beta, is limited to paid plans.
