OpenAI says it will stop using NDAs with public officials at its data centers.

Per CNBC, SiliconANGLE and The Verge, the company will seek only narrow protections for security information, trade secrets or sensitive data. The news came two days after the US election, where voters rejected data centers in most local votes.

OpenAI will also publish construction plans and noise projections, disclose expected jobs and create a local compact for each project, with a contact for residents. It reaffirmed it pays the full cost of power and grid upgrades and urged lawmakers to turn these practices into law for the whole industry.
