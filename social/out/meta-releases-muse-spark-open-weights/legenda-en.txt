Meta released open weights for Muse Spark Open, an 816-billion-parameter model trained for coding and agents.

According to Meta, the model uses 42 billion active parameters per token, takes text, images, audio and video, and has a 1-million-token context window.

Together AI says its API costs $1.25 per million input tokens and $4.25 per million output tokens. AWS confirms the model on SageMaker JumpStart in two variants, and the weights can be downloaded from Hugging Face. It follows Muse Glimmer, a 30-billion-parameter model released in August.
