Google DeepMind launched AlphaGenome 2, which predicts how DNA changes in each human cell type.

Per Google DeepMind, the earlier model predicted the effects of genetic variants across whole tissues. AlphaGenome 2 is conditional: it takes the cell type as part of its input and predicts one tissue, cell type or state at a time, which captures subtle differences between similar cells.

The company says the model was trained on more than 107 million profiles from human and mouse cells, covering 68 tissues, and finds nearly twice as many variants that change gene expression. Researchers can use the AlphaGenome API for non-commercial work, and companies get a private preview on Google Cloud.
