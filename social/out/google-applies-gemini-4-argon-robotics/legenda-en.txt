Google DeepMind says Gemini 4 Argon scored 87% on ERQA, a physical-reasoning test for robots.

According to the company, the model beats every other frontier model on the benchmark. In demos, it controlled a Spot robot, mapped a human video onto a humanoid and found the odd ball among 12 in three weighings.

Google also says Argon generated over 4,500 simulated episodes from one photo, lifting a real robot's success rate from 56% to 75%. The results come from DeepMind's own blog post. DeepMind says one model could support most stages of building and testing a robot.
