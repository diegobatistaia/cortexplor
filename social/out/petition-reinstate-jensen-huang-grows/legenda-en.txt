The petition to bring Jensen Huang back to Nvidia now has nearly 180,000 signatures.

Per TechCrunch and CNBC Technology, the "Stand with Jensen" petition grew from 115,000 signatures on Saturday to nearly 180,000 on Monday. Elon Musk said on X that he signed it, and Garry Tan and Tim Sweeney also confirmed their support, according to both outlets.

The petition's text says it is concerned that Huang was removed without due process and asks the board to bring him back while an independent investigation establishes the facts. Nvidia is keeping Colette Kress as interim CEO, and the board continues the investigation and the search for a permanent CEO, per both outlets. A spokesperson said no candidate has been engaged.
