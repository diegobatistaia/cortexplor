A flaw in SpaceXSI's Grok Build left data from thousands of users open to the public.

Per Ars Technica, Engadget and TechRadar, Claroc found 43,601 addresses of apps built in the tool, and more than 35,000 were reachable without a login. The random part of the link sat left of the domain, which let passive DNS tools list the addresses without having the original link.

The data included 24 SpaceXSI apps and information from other organizations, per the outlets. Claroc told Engadget and TechRadar that about 2% carried high or critical risk. Per Ars Technica, the company responded only after Claroc tagged Elon Musk on X, and it removed only the links to its own apps.
