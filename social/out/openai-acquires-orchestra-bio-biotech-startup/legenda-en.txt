OpenAI bought Orchestra Bio, a startup that coordinates drug development with AI agents.

According to OpenAI and TechCrunch, Orchestra joins Rosalind, OpenAI's life sciences AI platform, which already counts Moderna and Novo Nordisk as customers. Deal terms were not disclosed.

OpenAI says the agents track studies and campaigns in a drug program so the human team can decide the next step. TechCrunch says the startup had raised about $20 million and sees the deal as a sign of big AI labs moving into specific industries.
