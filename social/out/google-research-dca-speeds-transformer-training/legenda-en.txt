Google Research built DeepCrossAttention, a technique that promises faster AI training.

According to Google Research, DCA replaces the equal sum of earlier layers with a module that learns which ones matter most for each piece of text. The company says this reduces information dilution in deep networks.

On language tests, DCA matched a standard transformer with far fewer training steps, and one model needed fewer layers to reach a lower perplexity (Google Research). The company also says it can be applied to models that are already trained. It is research, not a product. The full story is at the link in bio.
