Hugging Face now accepts billing through Google Cloud accounts, with no credit card.

Per the company itself, the change covers paid services such as private hosting, Jobs on GPU, Inference Endpoints, Inference Providers and GPU Spaces. The process starts on Google Cloud Marketplace, where an admin subscribes to the Hugging Face Hub offer and picks the project and billing account.

Then the admin links the organization on Hugging Face. Per the company, every subscription goes through team review before billing starts through this channel, and card holders switch at the next billing cycle. Hugging Face says the change does not alter pricing and spares teams from registering a corporate card.
