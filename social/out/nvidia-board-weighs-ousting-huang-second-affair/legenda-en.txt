Nvidia's board met to decide whether to oust Jensen Huang, and shares fell.

Per CNBC, The Guardian, The Verge, TechCrunch and SiliconANGLE, the meeting followed a new New York Times report about the CEO. Shares fell 7% to 9% after hours, after the Wall Street Journal said the board was preparing to remove him.

The Guardian says analysts now treat an exit as the likely outcome. The board has 11 members, all independent but Huang, and the bylaws let it remove him by majority vote. Per TechCrunch and Bloomberg, an outside consultancy is already seeking possible replacements. The newspaper's claims are attributed and unconfirmed.
