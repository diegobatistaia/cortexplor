OpenAI stands by firing three safety researchers and denies retaliation, per CNBC and The Verge.

The researchers are Jasmine Wang, Tomek Korbak and Mikita Balesni. According to CNBC and Olhar Digital, all three had posted on X in September, calling for labs to slow frontier development or flagging safety concerns.

Tecmundo reports their letter is titled "OpenAI Cannot Make AI Safe Alone" and that Korbak highlighted his role in the internal investigation of the Hugging Face incident. OpenAI said it regretted how the case unfolded, per Tecmundo.

OpenAI says it agrees with the letter on preserving the monitorability of frontier models, CNBC reported.
