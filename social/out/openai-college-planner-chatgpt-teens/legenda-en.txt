OpenAI adds College Planner to ChatGPT for Teens, a tool for US high schoolers applying to college.

According to OpenAI, ChatGPT for Teens is the default experience applied automatically to accounts identified as belonging to someone under 18. It says the product includes built-in reminders to take a break.

OpenAI also says nearly half of teens who get a break reminder take a break or end the conversation within five minutes. It says the support for Boston Children's Hospital's Digital Wellness Lab will run three years. The figures are the company's own.

OpenAI says the planner handles logistics, while human advisers help students decide.
