Dario Amodei rejects the idea that Anthropic wants to cede control to AI and calls it absurd.

In a lengthy X post, the CEO said legitimacy and self-determination should never be sacrificed for efficiency or security, according to TechCrunch and Computer Hoy. He separates automating specific tasks from a broad surrender of human free will.

On recursive self-improvement, he acknowledged that limits may be needed. Amodei also accused rivals of using human-control language without changing their products (TechCrunch). Per Computer Hoy, the post drew close to 7 million views. The full story is at the link in bio.
