Microsoft will cut OneDrive in the 365 Family plan from 6 TB to a shared 2 TB and open Copilot to five more members.

The change was detailed in a notice to subscribers and first reported by The Verge this week, according to Canaltech and Tecmundo. Each of up to six people on an account had 1 TB before.

Tecmundo reports Microsoft promised temporary storage bonuses and transition support for the most affected subscribers. Age-based limits on content generation stay, and parents manage them in the family safety dashboard.

Canaltech adds that files above the new quota will not be deleted right away.
