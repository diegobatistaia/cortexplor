Meta rolled out Muse voice mode and video chat with its AI avatar to everyone in the US.

Per The Verge and TechCrunch, the avatar reacts with expressions and gestures while it talks. The default is called Jolly and can become another look, from a person to an animal. Voice tone changes by asking the agent or in settings. The avatar does not use the camera: it only listens to what is said.

TechCrunch says the feature runs on two new models, Muse Realtime Avatar and Muse Realtime Voice, and also works on the web and Mac. Before, only a limited group in the US had access. Meta says the feature is coming soon to its AI glasses and to the Muse Charm, a wearable that has not launched yet.
