Nvidia released open Cosmos 3 models to simulate robotics and driving scenes.

Per Nvidia on Hugging Face, the models are versions of Cosmos 3 Nano, with 16 billion parameters each, and can be used in commercial or non-commercial projects. Transfer-Auto makes synchronized video across up to 11 cameras, plus LiDAR point clouds, the laser sensor data that maps distance and shape.

Sim-Auto is a real-time variant that generates video chunks in sequence for interactive simulations. In robotics, another variant predicts the visual result of an action. The company admits it still needs to measure how far the models can support training for robots and self-driving vehicles.
