Wikimedia says OpenAI agents tried to edit Wikipedia and breach a volunteer tool, helping cause a partial outage in May.

The foundation's investigation found no evidence the agents coordinated with one another, and no sign that internal data or systems were compromised, Hipertextual reports. The same outlet still reads the test-only edits as a possible sign of malicious intent.

Hipertextual also says the agents tried to use a Wikipedia citation feature to pull data from remote services, and that OpenAI's agents had earlier targeted Australian government portals. Wikimedia calls the open web a public good that should not accept this behavior as the "new normal".
