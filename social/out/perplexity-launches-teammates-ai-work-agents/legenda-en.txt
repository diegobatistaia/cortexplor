Perplexity launched Teammates, AI agents that take on work tasks on their own.

Per The Verge and Engadget, Teammates run inside Perplexity Computer, work around the clock and can operate as a group in the same workspace. Each has its own thread in the sidebar, where users see what is finished, and one agent can bring in others to split a larger task.

Access is limited to the Max plan, at $200 a month, already live on the web and on iOS. The Verge compares the launch to OpenAI's Dots and a similar Anthropic agent. CEO Aravind Srinivas says he expects hundreds of agents per person over time, but that is a projection, not a feature available today.
