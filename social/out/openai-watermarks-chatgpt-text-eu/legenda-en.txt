OpenAI will embed an invisible watermark in ChatGPT and Codex text for users in the European Union to meet the AI Act.

The move comes two months after Anthropic said it would watermark text generated by Claude, also for the AI Act. Anthropic applies it worldwide, while OpenAI starts with the EU only, according to TechCrunch and The Verge.

OpenAI says the watermark does not identify the user, and it published benchmark scores showing similar performance with and without it (The Verge). Outside Europe, ChatGPT output is unchanged.
