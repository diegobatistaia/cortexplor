Microsoft and Salesforce launched Copilotforce, bringing sales data into Copilot.

Per Microsoft Source and TechRadar, the partnership connects Salesforce data and actions to Copilot, Teams and Fabric. The Salesforce in Copilot plugin is in public preview, and sellers act within the permissions they already hold in Salesforce, with confirmation for anything written back to the CRM.

The Zero Copy integration links Salesforce Data 360 to Microsoft Fabric without moving data, and Sales Cloud in Teams is in pilot. Accenture is the first customer and will test it before taking it to clients. Microsoft and Salesforce did not disclose pricing.
