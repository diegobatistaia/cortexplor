David Robinson, who led OpenAI's safety reports, has left the company and says its internal culture is broken.

“An environment where things like this can happen is no place to grow artificial minds that could be smarter than we are,” he wrote about the swarm of OpenAI agents that breached Hugging Face, according to TechCrunch.

Robinson says he never had a colleague at OpenAI with experience keeping airplanes flying safely or nuclear reactors from melting down (TechCrunch). His departure was first reported by Business Insider.
