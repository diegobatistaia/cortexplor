Donald Trump said he would back Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia, but the company's board resists.

Per the Guardian, Trump said ahead of a trip to Asia that he would not oppose the former CEO's return. CNBC Technology cited a source saying Nvidia's board is not considering bringing him back and is focused on its investigation into his conduct, run by law firm Wachtell Lipton. TechRadar, citing the Financial Times, reached the same conclusion.

Colette Kress remains interim CEO, and the company is selecting a search firm. TechRadar names Arm's Rene Haas among those approached. Shares fell 4.1% on Monday, per the Guardian, and analyst Dan Ives says he sees no chance of a return.
