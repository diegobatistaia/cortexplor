OpenAI says AI agents now generate 55% of output tokens from enterprise customers in the European Union.

According to OpenAI and TechRadar, agentic use of Codex and ChatGPT Work ranges from 22% in Slovenia to 85% in Lithuania. TechRadar cautions that the figure measures token volume, not how many companies adopted agents.

OpenAI says weekly use of these tools among EU small and medium businesses grew more than eightfold since May. Even so, the bloc's frontier firms are just 10% of the global group, versus 65% for North America, per the report.
