Nvidia and Microsoft unveiled hardware and Windows software to run AI agents as local AI on PCs, not in the cloud.

Acer, Asus, Dell, HP, Lenovo, MSI and Gigabyte are building their own RTX Spark machines, according to Nvidia. The chip delivers up to one petaflop of FP4 AI throughput, the company says.

Microsoft is rebuilding the Copilot app into a single interface woven through Windows, Fortune reports. Microsoft 365 Copilot passed 30 million paid seats by July, with new signups doubling each quarter, a figure the company gave Fortune. Google, OpenAI and Anthropic currently lead on frontier models, the outlet notes.
