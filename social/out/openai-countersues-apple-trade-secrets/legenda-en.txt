OpenAI countersues Apple and accuses it of trade secret theft.

According to the Guardian and Engadget, the filing was made in federal court in California and says Apple pressured hardware suppliers not to work with OpenAI. The company also claims Apple rehired a former employee who allegedly carried plans for a new device back to Apple.

Apple denies the claims and calls the filing an attempt to distract from the case it opened itself in July (The Guardian). Neither case has a ruling, and the ChatGPT partnership on iPhone continues (Engadget). The full story is at the link in bio.
