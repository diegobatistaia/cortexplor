Anthropic launched Cyber Mission, an initiative using AI to defend critical infrastructure, per Anthropic and Tecmundo.

Cyber Mission grows out of Project Glasswing, an earlier vulnerability-scanning program. Anthropic says it merged Glasswing that same week into an expanded Cyber Verification Program, which gives more defenders access to its most capable models.

Since June, Anthropic says it has offered Claude models to US state, local and tribal governments, now covering more than half of US states. For outside projects, it will keep sharing only human-verified disclosures (Anthropic).
