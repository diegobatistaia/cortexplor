Voters in at least ten US states approved limits on AI data centers in the elections.

Per CNBC and Engadget, of the local votes on the issue only Harwood, North Dakota, rejected the restriction. In Ohio, Sherrod Brown beat Jon Husted, whom he called the face of data centers, and in Texas Gina Hinojosa beat Greg Abbott.

An NBC News poll cited by CNBC shows 56% of voters saw data centers and AI as an important factor. Projects by OpenAI, SoftBank and Nvidia depend on local approval, and which ones get delayed is not yet decided.
