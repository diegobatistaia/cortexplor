Anthropic will ban users who are persistently cruel to Claude, under a revised usage policy effective November 12.

Canaltech notes Anthropic tested a narrower version in August 2025, when Claude Opus 4 and 4.1 gained the ability to end conversations judged extremely harmful. Until now, that tool was used only in exceptional situations.

Olhar Digital reports Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman answered the news with a melting-face emoji, and recalls Sam Altman defending the processing cost of polite chatbot messages. The same revision also bars use of Claude in disinformation, election interference, unauthorized surveillance and weapons development.
