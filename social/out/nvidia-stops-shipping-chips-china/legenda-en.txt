Nvidia stopped shipping licensed Hopper H200 chips to China.

Per The Verge and Olhar Digital, interim CEO Colette Kress said on the earnings call that guidance no longer includes licensed Hopper products for China and that the company stopped shipping them. She did not explain the reason. The channel had existed since December 2025, when the White House approved an exception to export restrictions.

The move came the same week as a new Commerce Department rule against indirect access through third-country data centers. Jefferies estimates, per TechCrunch, that the rules could cut $44 billion to $62 billion from Nvidia's data center revenue next year.
