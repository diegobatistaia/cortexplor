Anthropic launched Claude Haiku 5.5, its smallest Claude 5.5 model, now on Amazon Bedrock at about 75% lower cost.

Asana ran Haiku 5.5 through the evaluation suite of its AI Teammates agent ahead of launch. Task completion latency dropped more than 30%, and each agent turn ran up to two and a half times faster, SiliconANGLE reports.

The comparison with GPT-6 Luna relies on benchmarks Anthropic picked and ran itself. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, a coding test, Haiku 5.5 reportedly scored 39.2% against 16.4%. Anthropic also estimates the Sonnet 5.5 cache cut trims about a fifth off most agentic workloads, per SiliconANGLE.
