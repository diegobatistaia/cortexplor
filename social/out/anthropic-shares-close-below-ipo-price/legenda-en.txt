Anthropic shares closed below the IPO price.

According to CNBC, Anthropic shares fell 6.5% on Tuesday and closed below the $83 IPO price. TechCrunch saw the stock as low as $76 midday. The drop came as other AI stocks such as Nvidia, AMD and Intel also fell (CNBC Technology).

According to TechCrunch, trading below the IPO price is usually read as a negative sign, and the stock may serve as a gauge for the sector. Even so, three banks began coverage with favorable ratings, and Canaccord sees a $135 price target (CNBC Technology). A price target is an analyst's opinion, not a guarantee.
