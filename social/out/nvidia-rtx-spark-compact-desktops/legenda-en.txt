Nvidia launches RTX Spark mini desktops to run AI locally.

According to a Nvidia statement, the RTX Spark compact desktops, built by Asus, Dell and MSI, have been on sale since November 18 (NVIDIA). The company says they run AI agents, generate images and video and fine-tune models without the cloud, with a 20-core Grace CPU and up to 128GB of unified memory.

Nvidia says running models locally avoids per-token charges and keeps code and files on the machine, and a feature called Switchyard decides what stays local and what goes to the cloud (NVIDIA). It also cites native Adobe support for Windows on Arm and integration with Blender and ComfyUI.
