Anthropic launched Personal Finance, which lets Claude analyze spending with read-only access.

Per TechCrunch, The Verge, ZDNet and Engadget, the tool links bank and card accounts via Plaid. Claude sees balances, limits and up to two years of transactions, but cannot move or invest the money.

It totals spending by category, flags duplicate charges and builds charts. Anthropic says chats in the panel do not train its models and there are no ads. The beta is for Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers in the US, months after ChatGPT Finances.
