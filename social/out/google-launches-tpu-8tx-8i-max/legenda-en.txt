Google launched the TPU 8tx and TPU 8i Max, new chips for the era of AI agents.

Per Canaltech and Tecmundo, the TPU 8tx was designed to keep sensitive data and model weights isolated even during use, with security built into the hardware. It uses custom AMD CPU hosts and has half the latency of the TPU 8t.

The TPU 8i Max was built to remove memory bottlenecks in AI agents and doubles the compute, SRAM and memory bandwidth of the TPU 8i. Tecmundo reports that about 100 customers already use Google TPUs. The sources gave no pricing or general availability date for the new chips.
