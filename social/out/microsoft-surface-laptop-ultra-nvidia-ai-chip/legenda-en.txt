Microsoft unveiled the Surface Laptop Ultra, its first laptop built around Nvidia's RTX Spark chip, at $2,599.

Pricing climbs with memory: Hipertextual listed a 64GB configuration at $4,699. Dell also revealed its XPS 16 Creator Edition with the same Nvidia chip, priced at $3,800, according to TechCrunch.

It is not Microsoft's first push to tie hardware to AI. In 2024 it introduced the Copilot+ PC standard, which required a dedicated AI chip (CNBC). A new Windows feature, Hybrid Intelligence, lets Copilot use files stored on the PC to carry out tasks, according to The Verge and Engadget.

For local AI work, which matters more in a laptop like this: memory, price or the chip itself?
