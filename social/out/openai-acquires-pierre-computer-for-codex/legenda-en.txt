OpenAI announced it is acquiring Pierre Computer, a code storage startup for AI agents.

Per OpenAI and SiliconANGLE, Pierre's storage service will be built into Codex while the Trees and Diffs libraries stay open source. Tecmundo reports that the price is still undisclosed and that the transaction remains in progress, with no closing date given by either company.

Tecmundo and SiliconANGLE read the deal as a way for OpenAI to rely less on Microsoft's GitHub. The storage integration is expected in 2027, and the deal adds no new feature for Codex users yet.
