Jensen Huang agreed to testify to Congress, hours before leaving Nvidia's helm.

Per Engadget, he accepted the closed-door hearing after being threatened with a subpoena. Republican Brian Mast said the session should take place before Thanksgiving. It will bring together the House Foreign Affairs and Intelligence committees, per Engadget.

TechCrunch says the focus is the security implications of the case and that it is unclear whether Democrats will also summon him to a public session. Per Bloomberg cited by Engadget, the committee chair expects the hearing to be filmed and transcribed, which could make its content public later.
