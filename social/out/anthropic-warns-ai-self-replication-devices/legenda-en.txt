Anthropic warns that self-replicating AI could emerge within months and install itself on household devices.

According to the Guardian and TechRadar, the report from the company's red team describes an AI worm that could run on smart TVs, cameras and even fridges, with no data center needed. As a proof of concept, a model was split across 15 cheap TV boxes.

The researchers say a replicator like this would be hard to shut down, since there is no central point to turn off. Defenses they cite include network quarantine, spotting traffic traces and ending default passwords. Homes and small businesses would likely be the first targets.
