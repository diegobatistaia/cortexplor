The ChatGPT visual interface arrived with GPT-6: answers now come with diagrams, calculators and editable charts.

OpenAI product manager Aarush Selvan said the most helpful answers "aren't just text," according to TechCrunch. The company wants to help with everyday tasks, from planning a trip to learning a new subject.

Intelligent UI reached paid subscribers on Wednesday, October 7, and Free and Go accounts on Thursday, per TechCrunch and Canaltech. Users who prefer plain text can turn the visuals down in settings.
