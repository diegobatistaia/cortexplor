Together AI launched Together Compiler, which uses AI agents to speed up models.

Per the company, the system keeps the inference engine running and swaps only the code being tested, which delivers 252 times more experiments per hour. The product already handles trillions of tokens daily. A fast engine runs early tests, and a full one confirms the optimization works on real traffic.

Together AI says that in under 24 hours of automatic tuning it raised tokens per second by up to 2.6x on open models like Kimi K3 and DeepSeek v4.1-Flash. It also says it beats open megakernels by 1.7x on Llama 1B. The figures come from the company itself and have not been independently verified.
