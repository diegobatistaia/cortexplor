Trump suggested Apple's Tim Cook as Nvidia's new CEO, hours after Huang resigned.

Per TechCrunch and Hipertextual, the US president posted on Truth Social that only one man could take the job, and described the swap as one legend replacing another. Cook stepped down as Apple CEO on Sept. 1 after 15 years and is now executive chairman, per TechCrunch.

TechCrunch says it is unclear whether Trump truly backs the idea, and that the suggestion does not change Nvidia's official succession process. Hipertextual notes Cook is not a product-focused executive, as Huang was, and that there is no sign he wants to leave Apple.
