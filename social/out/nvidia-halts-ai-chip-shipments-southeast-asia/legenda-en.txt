Nvidia has paused AI chip shipments to Southeast Asia, according to Engadget and TechCrunch.

Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Indonesia are cut off from new servers carrying the company's chips, and partners like Dell and Supermicro told customers. Nvidia is checking whether regional partners meet US export rules.

The backdrop is a loophole: Chinese firms rented capacity from regional data centers to reach advanced chips remotely (TechCrunch AI). The US now gives those data centers 90 days to apply for a license (Engadget), and operators complain about the lack of guidance. There is no date for shipments to resume.
