ChatGPT now handles search volume equal to 20% of Google's, according to OpenAI.

Per The Verge and Olhar Digital, Adam Fry shared the figure at an OpenAI event. The company counts as search any query that triggers a web search, not simple questions. The source of Google's volume used in the comparison was not disclosed.

The Verge notes Bing reached 3% of the market in over a decade. OpenAI is betting on more visual answers, Intelligent UI, already shows sponsored images and weighs going public from 2027. The company did not say whether the growth will continue.
