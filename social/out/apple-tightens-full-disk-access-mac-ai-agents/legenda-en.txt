Apple will require explicit user action before apps get Full Disk Access, full access to the Mac, citing AI agents.

According to Apple, “as AI agents become increasingly capable and autonomous, the risks associated with this level of access will grow substantially.” The line was quoted by TechCrunch, The Verge and Ars Technica.

TechCrunch corrected its own story: the change is about informed consent and does not impose a new technical cap on what the permission can do. Apple did not name Meta or any other developer.
