Meta brings Muse, its personal AI agent, to Australia.

Meta announced that Muse is arriving in Australia with local partners in shopping, real estate, fitness and entertainment, including Canva, Domain, Kogan, MECCA, Officeworks and Ticketek, according to the company (Meta Newsroom). It is the third confirmed market, after the United States and Singapore.

According to Meta, each partner's features roll out progressively, and sensitive actions such as purchases happen only after the user confirms (Meta Newsroom). The company also released real-time voice chat and Spaces, custom environments. Meta did not say whether Muse reaches other markets.
