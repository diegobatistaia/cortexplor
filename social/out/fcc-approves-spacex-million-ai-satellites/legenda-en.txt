The FCC approved up to 1 million AI satellites for SpaceX.

The FCC authorized SpaceX to launch up to 1 million satellites with AI chips, according to CNBC, The Verge and SiliconANGLE. SpaceX describes the Starmind constellation as solar-powered data centers in orbit, with data sent to Earth by lasers tied to Starlink (The Verge AI).

Amazon, Viasat and astronomers tried to block the project, and the FCC rejected the petitions (CNBC Technology, SiliconANGLE). According to SiliconANGLE, missing any deadline voids the license, and the full 1 million is due by 2040. Google has already launched AI chips to space on a SpaceX rocket (CNBC Technology).
