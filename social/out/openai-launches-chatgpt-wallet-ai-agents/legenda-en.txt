OpenAI launched ChatGPT Wallet, a wallet for Dots agents to shop.

Per CNBC and the Verge, the feature starts limited to ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the United States. The agent finds the product but only completes the purchase after a security check on every transaction, which can be biometric, per the Verge.

The agent never sees the real card number, only a payment token, per CNBC. OpenAI takes no commission in this early phase. CNBC notes Amazon already blocks shopping agents and United Airlines will block bookings by agents. The company says agent commerce is still at an early stage.
