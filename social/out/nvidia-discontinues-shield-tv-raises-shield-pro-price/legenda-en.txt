Nvidia discontinued the Shield TV and raised the Shield TV Pro price by 50%, from $199 to $299.

Launched in 2019, the Shield TV Pro plays 4K HDR video with Dolby Vision and uses AI to upscale content to 4K at 60 frames per second, according to TechRadar.

Wired recalls that Nvidia executive Andrew Bell once called the Shield a “passion project” inside the company. For the outlet, the device still makes sense mainly for buyers who need its broad codec support or a Plex server. TechRadar adds that a new Apple TV is reportedly expected soon, so shoppers may want to compare first.
