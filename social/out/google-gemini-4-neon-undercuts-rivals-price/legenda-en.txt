Google launched Gemini 4 Neon, a fast, lower-priced model aimed at businesses.

Per Google DeepMind, Neon sits between Carbon, the most powerful, and Hydrogen, the cheapest. SiliconANGLE reports it runs about three times faster than Carbon at 60% lower cost per task, and costs $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens.

On Artificial Analysis, Neon scored 61 points and placed third, behind Claude Fable 5.2 and GPT-6.2 Astra, per SiliconANGLE. The context window reaches 1 million tokens, and a cybersecurity version will have limited access, per Olhar Digital IA.
