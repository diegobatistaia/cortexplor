Nvidia launched Nemotron 3.5 Super VL, an open 120-billion-parameter model, plus two Bolt models.

According to Nvidia, SiliconANGLE and ZDNet, Bolt 74B is available now and fits in 24GB of memory with 2-bit quantization. The 4GB Bolt E4B arrives Oct. 23.

Nvidia says Super VL runs about five times faster than the next-best model in its class, based on Artificial Analysis testing cited by ZDNet. Weights, data and training recipes will be open, and organizations can run the models on their own infrastructure. Nvidia also packages Super VL as a production-ready microservice.
