Meta brought Muse, its personal AI agent, to Singapore, with local shopping and travel partners.

Per Meta, the agent links to Gmail, Google Calendar and Instagram, plus services like Shopee, Grab, Agoda and Singapore Airlines. It can compare prices, line up food orders and handle travel bookings.

The company says Muse runs on a cloud virtual machine that keeps passwords and payment data away from the agent, and it checks with the person before sending an email or buying. Connectors roll out step by step, and Meta has not said whether Muse reaches other markets.
