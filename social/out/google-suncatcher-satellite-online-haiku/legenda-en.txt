Google's Project Suncatcher satellite answered its first command from Earth with a haiku.

Per TechCrunch and The Verge, the prototype is online and sent the poem, written by the Gemma 4 model. The craft rode a SpaceX Falcon 9 and carries four TPUs, but because of its power limit it runs only one at a time, for about 20 minutes, per TechCrunch.

The next phase measures heat and radiation, which can corrupt data without tripping alarms, according to TechCrunch. If results hold, Google says it will launch two more satellites in 2027, with optical links between them. TechCrunch notes the project should only pay off in the 2030s.
