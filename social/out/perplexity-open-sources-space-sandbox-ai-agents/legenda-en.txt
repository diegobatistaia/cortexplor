Perplexity open-sourced SPACE, the sandbox that isolates the AI agents behind its Computer product.

According to Perplexity, each agent runs in its own virtual machine, with network access and credentials kept outside the environment. The company says SPACE has run every Computer session since July.

Perplexity says that across more than a hundred escape attempts in testing, no agent fully got out, and that network gaps found were fixed. According to the company and SiliconANGLE, CoreWeave, Akamai and Crusoe are already testing or integrating the tool.
