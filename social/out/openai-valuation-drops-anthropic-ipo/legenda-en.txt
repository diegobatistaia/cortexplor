OpenAI was valued at $1.25 trillion in its new round, short of the $1.4 trillion it sought.

Per TechCrunch and The Verge, the round has raised $11 billion so far and could grow to $20 billion. The news came days after Anthropic filed its S-1 to go public, with no offering date yet. The estimate for the rival fell from $2 trillion to a range of $1.2 to $1.3 trillion.

Behind the revisions is a slowdown in AI spending: Ramp data shows growth of just 1.5% from August to September, after 42% in July, then a rebound of 8.9% in October. Ramp's economist blames price cuts by the model makers themselves, not a lack of demand.

Is the AI boom cooling or just getting cheaper?
