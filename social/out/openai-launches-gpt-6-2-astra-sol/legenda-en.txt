OpenAI launched GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol for paying ChatGPT subscribers.

Per TechRadar, SiliconANGLE and Tecmundo, Astra targets complex professional projects and Sol everyday tasks. Both are in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API, for Plus, Pro, Enterprise and Edu plans, and free accounts do not get access.

OpenAI says Astra scored 5.2 points above GPT-6 Astra on Agents' Last Exam, at under a third of the cost per task. The company also says the models hit its highest cybersecurity risk level, so they are not yet in Daybreak, its program for security researchers.
