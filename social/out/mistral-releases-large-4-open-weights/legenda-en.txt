Mistral opened the weights for Large 4, a multimodal model with 1 trillion parameters, per Hugging Face.

Per Hugging Face and Mistral AI, the weights went live on October 29 under an MIT-derived license that limits model-as-a-service offerings. Large 4 takes text and images, generates text and has a 1-million-token context window, according to Hugging Face.

Mistral AI claims it leads Terminal-Bench 4.0 among open models, 13 points above its preview, and uses 47% fewer tokens per task than GLM-5.3. It runs on transformers, vLLM and llama.cpp from launch, including GGUF builds for lighter hardware, per Hugging Face.
