OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said the AI industry will be fine without Jensen Huang at Nvidia.

Per TechCrunch AI and Olhar Digital IA, it was Altman's first public comment on Huang's exit, made at Semafor's Silicon Valley & The World event. Asked whether the founder should return as CEO, he said he doesn't know enough to weigh in.

Altman tied his optimism to strong AI demand and pointed to Huang's ambition as a driver for the sector. OpenAI depends on Nvidia, which gave a guarantee of up to $105 billion for its Ohio data center, per both outlets, and Altman said he isn't worried about that financing. Nvidia remains under interim CEO Colette Kress.
