Eight US states sued OpenAI, accusing ChatGPT of harming children and misleading parents.

According to CNBC and The Verge, Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, West Virginia, Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho and Montana filed separate suits. They allege violations of consumer-protection laws and data collection from minors. These are accusations, not yet decided in court.

The states seek civil penalties and an end to what they call misleading claims. OpenAI says it has age-specific protections, such as parental controls and break reminders, and wants to work with states on rules for the whole industry.
