Anthropic debuted on the Nasdaq and shares opened 34% above the IPO price.

Per CNBC Technology and Fortune, the maker of Claude began trading under the ticker ANT and opened at $111.50, pushing its market value to roughly $2 trillion. The IPO was priced at $83 a share, above the $70-to-$78 range.

The offering raised $81.3 billion, the largest on record by funds raised, ahead of SpaceX's $75 billion, per CNBC Technology. The company posted $33.24 billion in revenue over nine months but a $104 billion net loss, attributed mostly to accounting changes, per Fortune.
