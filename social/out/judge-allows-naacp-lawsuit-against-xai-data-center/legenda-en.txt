A US judge denied xAI's bid to end the NAACP lawsuit over its Colossus data center.

Per CNBC and TechCrunch, xAI wanted the case thrown out. It accuses the company of running gas turbines without emissions permits near Memphis. Judge Debra Brown rejected both of its arguments and let the case move forward.

TechCrunch notes the same court had already denied a similar motion from the Justice Department. The outlets differ on the turbine count: CNBC cites 27, TechCrunch at least 35. The case also lands amid growing US backlash against AI data centers.
