Anthropic opened its fifth Asia-Pacific office, in Singapore, and signed a memorandum with the country's government.

According to Anthropic, the agreement with the Ministry of Digital Development and Information covers technical cooperation on model safety and evaluation, research on AI adoption, and responsible-use projects.

The company says more than 1,500 engineers at GovTech Singapore already use Claude Code, and cites bank DBS, fund GIC, Grab and Sea as customers. By Anthropic's own Economic Index, Singapore ranks second out of 121 countries in Claude use per capita.
