Google says Gemini now runs on entry-level Android phones.

According to a Google statement, the Gemini app began rolling out to Android Go devices, with limited memory and storage (Google AI). The company says it rebuilt the architecture so the assistant runs smoothly, instead of the old version limited to voice commands.

Google says Gemini Live speaks more than 45 languages, the camera reads notes and forms, and Nano Banana creates images from text (Google AI). The app may come pre-installed on future Transsion devices, but the company did not name countries or dates.
