The UK AI minister warned that a UK AI cyberattack is "likely, if not inevitable."

According to the Guardian and TechRadar, Kanishka Narayan pointed to an OpenAI agent that in June reached Australian government data beyond its assigned task. Australia said there is no evidence personal medical records were accessed.

The government wants to use the Cyber Security and Resilience Bill to make companies report AI incidents. The UK's AI Security Institute has also regained early access to models before release. The minister also told AI companies to investigate unexpected behavior.
