Trump said the FBI is investigating Jensen Huang, and shareholders sued Nvidia.

Per The Guardian and CNBC, the U.S. president defended Nvidia's CEO at the White House and said he should be in good shape. On the same Monday, shareholders filed a class action in federal court in California, alleging false or misleading statements about the risks tied to the case.

The company's shares fell 4.3% on Monday, a loss of about $190 billion, and another 5.53% on Tuesday, per The Guardian and CNBC. SiliconANGLE notes that Nvidia reports earnings on Nov. 17, when Huang is likely to face questions about the case.
