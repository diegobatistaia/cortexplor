Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after he stepped down.

Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, president and board member, effective immediately, by unanimous board vote, according to the Guardian, CNBC and Wired. The move came 11 days after his exit and reversed the board chair's remarks that the company did not intend to bring him back (Wired AI, Engadget).

The company says it consulted senior US officials and concluded his return poses no national security risk (Wired AI). The independent investigation stays open, and shares jumped more than 30% on Monday (CNBC Technology, Xataka). The outlets don't say whether Trump swayed the board.
