Google will limit free Gemini to Flash-Lite from October 9, and the $4.99 Plus plan loses Gemini Pro as well.

Hipertextual calls it the biggest overhaul of Gemini since Google added a model picker to the app. Google had already introduced a dual rate limit, one cap resetting every 5 hours and another building over the week, the same structure Anthropic uses for Claude, and applied a similar restriction to NotebookLM.

The Pro and Ultra plans also differ on usage limits per model, cloud storage and bundled Google services (Hipertextual). Google keeps low, medium and high effort settings for each model; The Verge says high-effort Deep Think burns through limits faster.
