Meta announced university research funding: partnerships with five US schools and $2 million in total.

Per Meta, the package combines model access, funding and collaboration with company researchers. Caltech, Ohio State, LSU, Indiana University and UTHealth Houston are taking part, with a focus on medicine, physics, chemistry and other fields.

Projects named include an LSU computer-vision system to monitor infants with tracheostomies, Indiana AI for conversations with dementia patients and an Ohio State model to predict heart failure. Meta did not detail how the money splits or give a timeline for results.
