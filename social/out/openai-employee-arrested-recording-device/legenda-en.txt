OpenAI says an employee was arrested after planting a recording device in an executive room at its headquarters.

According to the Guardian and Fortune, the device sat behind a television, plugged into a USB port and hidden in toilet paper. Security found it on September 30. The company says it used its own AI models to trace where it came from.

The suspect was arrested on October 9 and faces a federal charge, with no conviction yet. OpenAI says it has no indication of access to model weights, source code or customer data. The FBI director called the case a counterintelligence priority.
