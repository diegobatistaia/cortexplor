Anthropic hired fewer junior staff and spent more time in meetings over the past year.

An internal Anthropic report, covered by TechCrunch and TechRadar, shows juniors fell from 21% to 14% of the technical workforce since 2025. Their share of new technical hires dropped from 27% to 9%.

TechRadar cites gains the company credits to AI, such as 5.1 times more commits per engineer. Meeting time rose about 20%, and the report points to human attention as the new bottleneck. The data comes from Anthropic itself and is framed, the company says, as a signal for other firms.

Should AI shrink the entry-level jobs for people just starting out?
