Microsoft built a real-time risk score to monitor AI agents inside companies.

According to Microsoft and SiliconANGLE, each agent gets an identity and a risk score that rises when it strays from what was intended. A high score can remove write access, require human approval or isolate the agent entirely.

SiliconANGLE says Microsoft's tools now detect 60 kinds of local agents, including Claude Code, Codex and GitHub Copilot. The company did not share pricing or a general availability date and promises more details at Microsoft Ignite.
