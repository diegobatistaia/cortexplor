Google links Wallet and Drive to Search personalization.

According to Google and TechCrunch, AI Mode's Personal Intelligence in Search now connects to Wallet, Drive and the Gemini app, on top of Gmail, Calendar and Photos (Google AI, TechCrunch AI). With Wallet, the AI uses spending history but only reads the account and cannot move money.

The feature is opt-in: each app is turned on manually and can be switched off whenever the user wants (Google AI). Wallet began rolling out Monday, and Drive and Gemini arrive in the coming weeks. TechCrunch AI notes Google holds personal data that search rivals lack.
