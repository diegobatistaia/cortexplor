Meta launched Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, the first frontier models from Meta Superintelligence Labs.

TechCrunch notes this is the first frontier-level release since Scale AI founder Alexandr Wang restructured Meta's AI efforts in August of last year. The models follow Muse Spark, which Meta describes as a lightweight model built for low latency.

Research scientist Kavya Kopparapu told TechCrunch the team put effort into making sure the model can remember what matters to a user over days, weeks and months. According to TechCrunch, training took place at Meta's Prometheus data center in Ohio.

Meta says developers can also use Wonder to find flaws in their code.
