Amazon released Mitra-v2, an open AI model for predicting values in data tables.

Per Amazon Science, the weights are on Hugging Face under the Apache 2.0 license, with classification and regression versions. The model reads labeled rows of a new table and predicts the rest, with no extra training, through in-context learning.

Amazon says Mitra-v2 was pretrained on synthetic tables with ten times more rows and about three times more columns than Mitra-v1. Per the company, it leads standalone models on TabArena and beat Mitra-v1 on 32 of 33 datasets. The Amazon Connect team already uses the model internally.
