xAI launched Grok Voice 3.0, which tops Artificial Analysis's voice ranking with a score of 86.9.

According to xAI and SiliconANGLE, OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 scored 83.2 and Google's Gemini 3.8 Live scored 82.6. On noisy, accented support calls, Grok trailed OpenAI slightly: 71.6% versus 74.5%.

The model costs about 2.5 cents per task on Big Bench Audio, versus 3 cents for GPT-Live-1. xAI says it already handles millions of conversations a day in Tesla cars and about 20,000 daily calls at Starlink. In the API, the price is $0.08 per minute.
