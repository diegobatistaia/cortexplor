OpenAI is merging ChatGPT's Chat and Work tabs into one experience, starting in December.

Per TechCrunch and ZDNet, a select group of users tests the new layout before a wider rollout. On the single screen, a user starts from an idea and asks ChatGPT to carry it forward, building a site, a presentation or web tasks.

ChatGPT and Codex will share one token-based usage pool, and OpenAI warns a minority may wait longer for limits to reset. Instant chat does not draw from the pool. Anthropic merged Claude's chat and Cowork in September, according to both outlets.
