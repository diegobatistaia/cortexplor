Apple shows a model of nearly 816 billion parameters running across several Mac Studios.

According to Apple Machine Learning, the new MLX version enables distributed inference: four Mac Studios run Muse Spark Open at over 36 tokens per second. It builds on an RDMA driver over Thunderbolt 5 and new jaccl communication collectives.

The company says the M5 Ultra produces the first token up to 4.6 times faster than the M3 Ultra. MLX is free and open source, and the cost sits in the hardware. All figures come from Apple's own tests.
