AMD's revenue rose 45% in the third quarter, driven by data centers and agentic AI.

Per CNBC and SiliconANGLE, AMD posted $13.38 billion in revenue and data centers became 64% of the total. The company says its processors are growing again with agentic AI apps, which run well on CPUs.

The fourth-quarter outlook is about $17 billion, above what analysts expected. Anthropic, OpenAI, Microsoft, Oracle and Meta already use Helios systems. PC and gaming chips lost revenue, and the sector fears overspending on AI. Lisa Su says demand should grow strongly.
