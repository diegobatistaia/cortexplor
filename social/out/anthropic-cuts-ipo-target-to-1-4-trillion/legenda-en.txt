Anthropic cut its IPO target to $1.4 trillion and landed $26 billion from tech giants.

Per Fortune and The Verge, the valuation fell from the roughly $2 trillion in earlier reports. Google, Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, Micron, Microsoft and TSMC Partners pledged to buy $26 billion in non-voting Class N shares in a private placement, per Fortune and Business Insider.

Adding the IPO, Fortune calculates that Anthropic could raise up to $90.4 billion. Business Insider notes the deal revives criticism of circular financing in the AI industry. Per Fortune, citing the Financial Times, the banks are already weighing a price above the filed range.
