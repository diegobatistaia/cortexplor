Nvidia's board opened an internal probe into Jensen Huang after pressure from Congress.

Per CNBC, three House committee chairs asked Huang for a closed-door briefing on November 18, a day after third-quarter earnings. TechCrunch and The Verge report that a US intelligence memo, first reported by The New York Times, alleges China recruited executive Alice Huang to get close to the CEO and gather information about AI chips.

Nvidia denies wrongdoing. A spokesperson said contacts with Alice Huang were not unusual and that the company does not share classified information with customers. Alice Huang is an executive at Megaspeed, a Nvidia customer, and the two are not related. The claims are unproven.
