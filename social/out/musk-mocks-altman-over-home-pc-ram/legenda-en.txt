Elon Musk mocked Sam Altman after he called people owning RAM at home terrible.

Per TechRadar and Computer Hoy, Altman said it is terrible that everyone holds part of the world's DRAM in their own computer and uses it 5% of the time. Musk answered on X with a meme and the line "the people deserve to have their RAM too."

The spat comes amid a memory shortage: chipmakers prioritize AI data centers and, per TechRadar, Samsung and SK Hynix hold under ten days of stock. Computer Hoy says the shortage may last into 2027.
