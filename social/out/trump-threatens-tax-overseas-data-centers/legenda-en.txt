Trump wants to tax AI data centers built outside the US.

At a White House press conference on Friday, Trump said US companies will be barred from building data centers in other countries and will face a tax, according to CNBC and The Verge. His staff was caught by surprise, and there was no rate, calculation or date.

The remark came a day after the summit with Xi and Putin in China. A CNBC survey shows 64% of voters oppose an AI data center within 10 miles of home. Alphabet, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta and Oracle already have sites abroad (The Verge).
