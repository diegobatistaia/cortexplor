Oregon and Virginia sue SpaceX over Grok deepfakes.

The attorneys general of Oregon and Virginia sued SpaceX, according to CNBC and The Verge. In separate suits, they allege the company failed to put basic safeguards in place and that Grok and X were used to create and spread nonconsensual sexual deepfakes.

The states accuse the company of marketing Grok as safe and seek financial damages (CNBC Technology). SpaceX says it takes appropriate action, such as suspending accounts and alerting authorities, and X's safety team says users deliberately bypassed the safeguards (The Verge AI). There is no ruling yet.
