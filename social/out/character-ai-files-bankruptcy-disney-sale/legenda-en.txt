Character AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in the US and lined up Disney as the lead bidder, with a $20 million offer.

According to The Verge and Engadget, the startup listed estimated liabilities of $50 million to $100 million. Disney's bid is the opening offer in the auction and could rise if others bid higher.

The company says it will keep services running, including paid subscriptions, while the sale is pending, expected between late November and early December. If no suitable buyer is found, the platform could be shut down.
