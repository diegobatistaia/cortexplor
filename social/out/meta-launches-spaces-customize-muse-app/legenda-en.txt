Meta launched the Muse spaces feature: users describe an idea and the app builds the dashboard.

Per Meta Newsroom, the feature lets users build custom dashboards inside Muse, such as a holiday gift tracker, a daily word game or a project board. Each space stays saved in the app, and the AI adjusts it on request, changing the look or behavior.

The Verge says spaces can connect to apps like Gmail, Google Calendar, OpenTable and Notion to update themselves. Meta also brings Active Memory and Active Focus to Muse, which help it recall earlier conversations. The company is exploring ways for users to share their spaces with each other.
