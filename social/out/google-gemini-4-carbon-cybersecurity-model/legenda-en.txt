Google DeepMind launched Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-focused model with access limited to defense teams.

Per DeepMind, Carbon tops the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 59 and hit 88.2% on SEC-bench Pro, a test of finding flaws in code. Those figures come from the company, not an independent measurement.

DeepMind says the model was built for long engineering tasks and keeps the launch price of Gemini 4 Argon, $2 per million input tokens. Access starts with cyber defenders and then reaches Fairwind Program partners, with no date for the general public.
