Jensen Huang resigned as Nvidia CEO, and shares fell more than 10%.

Per The Guardian, CNBC, The Verge, Wired, Engadget, TechRadar and Hipertextual, Huang left his posts as chairman, CEO and board member effective immediately, after two reports about his personal life. CFO Colette Kress takes over on an interim basis.

The board's statement does not mention the scandal, per The Guardian. Nvidia named Harvey C. Jones as board chairman and is searching for a permanent CEO, per CNBC. Shares fell more than 10%, below $4 trillion in market value, per CNBC.
