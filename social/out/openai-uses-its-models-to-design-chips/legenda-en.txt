OpenAI says it uses its models to improve chip design, including with AMD.

According to OpenAI itself, its models rewrite the code that describes circuits and use Synopsys tools to check that behavior is preserved (OpenAI). On Jalapeño, its first in-house inference chip, the company says it cut about 13% of the area in the vector unit and 10% in the matrix unit.

OpenAI says the optimizations were proven in silicon and that AMD has already included some of the changes in a recent design (OpenAI). The company also says it partnered with Synopsys to bring the models into chip design tools.
