Mistral released Le Chonk, or Mistral Large 4, a 1-trillion-parameter open-weight model, now in preview.

Mistral co-founder Guillaume Lample told Wired that owning a model matters even for US companies, since a closed model may not be available tomorrow. He said the lab targets areas larger labs tend to skip.

French President Emmanuel Macron called the release a third way in AI, alongside US and Chinese models (TechCrunch). Wired links Mistral's momentum to tighter US limits on how American companies distribute advanced models.
