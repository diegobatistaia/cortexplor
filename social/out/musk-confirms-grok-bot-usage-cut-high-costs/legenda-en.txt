Elon Musk confirmed he cut Grok Bot's weekly limits on purpose because of costs.

Per TechCrunch and TechRadar, users saw their weekly cap run out suddenly over Halloween weekend, without heavier use than usual. Musk replied on X that running costs were too high for the product's revenue.

TechCrunch says AI now makes up 52% of SpaceX revenue. TechRadar notes OpenAI has also quietly trimmed limits and reads the episode as a possible sign of more adjustments in the sector. Musk did not say how limits will look going forward.
