A wave of US outages reignites the debate over AI's role.

Microsoft's Outlook, Teams and Copilot went down, the FAA grounded flights nationwide and Apple Pay stopped working, according to TechCrunch and Wired. U.S. officials deny any cyberattack or link between the cases, and the companies point to technical causes.

Experts are split. Dave DeWalt sees growing pains of a security system getting stronger, while Anusha Iyer blames the rushed mix of legacy systems with new technology (TechCrunch). Senator Mark Warner said AI is moving faster than our ability to manage its consequences (Wired).
